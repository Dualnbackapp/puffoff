PuffOff

Quit vaping. Day by day.

PuffOff tracks the days you have held on, the money you have kept and the cravings you have logged — so that stopping becomes something you can see.

What it does

A day counter that survives a slip. The money you have saved, from your own price and quantity. A craving log that shows you when they hit. Milestones that arrive when you least expect them.

Your data
