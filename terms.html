What PuffOff is

PuffOff is a tracking and motivation app for people who want to stop vaping. It is not a medical device and provides no medical advice. If you have a health concern, talk to a healthcare professional.

Subscriptions

PuffOff offers subscriptions and a one-time purchase. Payment is charged to your Apple Account at confirmation. Subscriptions renew automatically unless cancelled at least 24 hours before the end of the period. Manage or cancel in Settings → your name → Subscriptions.
